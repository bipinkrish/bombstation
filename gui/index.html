How Plugins Load in BombSquad & Ballistica

BombSquad automatically scans and compiles all .py files in your mods directory. Plugins exported with # ba_meta export babase.Plugin are registered directly into the game engine's internal mod catalog.

Tip 1 Once installed, enable plugins in-game under Settings → Plugins.
Tip 2 For dedicated headless servers, plugins placed in the target mods directory are auto-loaded at startup.
